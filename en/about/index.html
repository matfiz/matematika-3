About this site

The companion to General Mathematics III — series, differential equations and vector calculus.

A resource for looking things up

General Mathematics has three parts of fourteen lectures. It is the first university mathematics for physics students, assuming school algebra and trigonometry.

Each lecture opens with its direct Uses and closes with a summary. The mathematical results retain their hypotheses and the proof status given in the notes.

The course is available in Lithuanian and English.

Proof policy

An unmarked result is proved; sketched gives the idea and key step; cited supplies a source. Results inherit a weaker tag, except when only standing facts are unproved ingredients.

Exercise sheets

Only questions are public. Hints, answers and solutions are removed before extraction and excluded from public PDFs.

Privacy
